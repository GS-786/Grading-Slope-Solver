Standards

Grading standards

The limits every solver checks against. Pick one here or in the toolbar, or type your own authority’s values in the last column.

Limits are summarised from the published documents for quick checking: 2010 ADA Standards for Accessible Design, IBC 2021, Approved Document M (England) and BS 8300-1. A dash means the standard sets no limit there, so typical practice is used and labelled “Typical practice”. Always confirm against the current edition and your authority’s requirements. This tool is not a substitute for a code review.
